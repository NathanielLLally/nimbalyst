# VolumeUp Agency — Roofing Lead Generation Analysis

## Executive Summary

**VolumeUp Agency** is a roofing-exclusive lead generation and digital marketing firm founded in 2019 by George Roros. The agency positions itself as an alternative to shared lead marketplaces (Angi, HomeAdvisor) and traditional "black box" agencies by building client-owned lead systems with transparent, sales-focused attribution.

**Core tagline:** "Stop Renting Leads. Build Your Own Lead Machine."

**Target:** Residential roofing contractors, storm damage specialists, and roofing companies scaling past $5M revenue with $10K+/month ad budgets.

**Key differentiator:** Optimization toward closed sales (cost per sale) rather than lead volume (cost per lead), which they call a "vanity metric."

---

## Company Overview

- **Founded:** 2019
- **Founder:** George Roros (visible public figure; no other staff mentioned)
- **Industry focus:** Roofing exclusively
- **Credentials:** Google Partner, Meta Business Partner
- **Approach:** Direct response advertising + lead system automation + weekly optimization calls

**Philosophy:** George founded the company out of frustration with roofing marketing dysfunction—contractors overpaying for shared leads resold to competitors, and agencies unable to tie ad spend to closed deals.

---

## Value Proposition

VolumeUp's core argument against three incumbent models:

| **Problem** | **VolumeUp Position** |
|---|---|
| **Shared lead marketplaces** (Angi, HomeAdvisor) | Leads are resold to 5–10 competitors; margin is eaten by the platform; no exclusivity. |
| **"Black box" agencies** | They report cost-per-lead but can't prove revenue attribution; spend is a mystery. |
| **"Order taker" agencies** | Execute instructions without strategy; no consultative partnership; no skin in the game. |

**VolumeUp's remedy:**
- Campaigns run in **client-owned ad accounts** (Meta, Google, YouTube, programmatic)
- **Attribution from click to closed sale** via UTM tracking + weekly sales team feedback
- **Weekly optimization calls** feeding sales disposition data back into ad targeting
- **Full client ownership** of assets—on departure, client retains ad accounts, pixel data, audiences, landing pages

## Service Offerings & Pricing

### 1. **Feedback Loop Lead System** (Flagship)

The agency's core offering—a multi-platform advertising system with sales-driven optimization.

**Platforms:**
- Meta (Facebook/Instagram)
- Google Demand Gen
- YouTube
- Programmatic display
- Google Local Service Ads (LSA)
- Google Search Ads

**Deliverables:**
- Setup of campaigns across multiple platforms
- High-converting landing pages + instant estimate funnels
- Ad-level UTM attribution (identifies which creative produces sales)
- CRM integration (GoHighLevel, JobNimbus) with unified dashboard
- Weekly sales team feedback loop feeding optimization
- Nurture/follow-up sequences embedded in client pipeline
- Weekly strategy + optimization calls

**Pricing:**
- **Setup fee:** $1,000 (one-time)
- **Management fee:** 30% of ad spend
- **Minimum monthly ad spend:** $10,000
- **All-in monthly estimate:** $13,000–$15,000/month
- **Contract:** Month-to-month, no long-term lock-in

**Ideal client:** $1M+ annual revenue, prepared for ongoing $10K+/month spend.

**Timeline:**
- Leads appear within 48–72 hours of campaign launch
- Weeks 1–4: data gathering, audience building, optimization
- Months 2–3: feedback loop runs on real sales data; optimization accelerates

---

### 2. **Unlimited Live Transfer System**

Pre-qualified leads transferred live to the client's sales team during business hours.

**How it works:**
- Facebook ads generate inbound leads
- In-house call team pre-qualifies every lead by phone
- Homeowner is transferred **live to client's sales team** while still on the phone
- Automatic re-booking for missed transfers
- Included tools: custom roofing CRM + pipeline tracking, regular strategy meetings

**Pricing:**
- Custom, based on market and volume
- Requires discovery call for specifics
- No fixed rate disclosed

**Differentiators:**
- Leads are exclusive (not resold)
- Pre-qualified by a real person (not algorithmic)
- Real-time transfer (not batch or email)
- Claimed to have "dramatically higher close rate" vs. shared platforms like Angi/HomeAdvisor

## Pricing Model Analysis

### Cost Structure
- **Setup:** $1K (amortized low)
- **Ad spend floor:** $10K/month (mandatory)
- **Management fee:** 30% of ad spend
- **All-in:** ~$13K–$15K/month

### Comparison to Alternatives

| **Model** | **Cost Example (at $10K ad spend)** | **Pro** | **Con** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **VolumeUp (% of spend)** | $3K (30%) + $1K setup = $4K first month, $3K ongoing | Scales with results; success-aligned | Higher margin for agency; eats into profit if ROAS is low |
| **Fixed CPL** (e.g., $50/lead) | $500–$2K/month (varies on volume) | Predictable cost per lead | No alignment if leads don't convert |
| **Retainer** (e.g., $2K–$5K fixed) | $2K–$5K/month | Stable costs; small up-front | Doesn't scale with performance; could be underutilized |
| **Shared lead platform** (Angi, HomeAdvisor) | $500–$2K/month | Very low entry; immediate flow | Leads resold; poor close rates; no exclusivity |

**VolumeUp's pricing model strengths:**
- Aligns agency incentive with client profitability (revenue, not leads)
- Transparent (% of spend tied to performance)

**VolumeUp's pricing model weaknesses:**
- 30% is a high agency margin (typical is 15–25%)
- $10K/month minimum excludes smaller operators
- No volume discount or cheaper tier

## Red Flags & Caveats

1. **Anonymized case studies** — Most don't name the client; limits verification and reference-checking.
2. **No guarantees** — Zero performance guarantees or SLAs; client shoulders all risk.
3. **Solo founder visibility** — George Roros is the only named operator; unclear if there's a support team, processes, or succession plan.
4. **ROAS claims** — 1,600%+ ROAS is exceptionally high; not typical in most industries or even roofing. Likely cherry-picked best performers. No mention of average client ROAS.
5. **Minimum spend** — $10K/month is steep; if ad spend is mismanaged, client still pays 30%.
6. **Results dependent on client execution** — Agency provides leads; client's sales team must close them. Agency can't control that; success claims may double-count client sales skill.
7. **No contractual guarantees** — Month-to-month is great for flexibility but means agency could be fired mid-campaign; no long-term commitment protection.

## Conclusion

**VolumeUp Agency is a specialized, performance-focused lead-gen firm that trades breadth for depth.** Its roofing-only focus, sales-driven attribution, and client asset ownership are genuine differentiators in a crowded lead-gen market. However, the high minimum spend, lack of performance guarantees, anonymized case studies, and pricing model (30% of ad spend) create trade-offs.

**Best positioned against:**
- Shared lead platforms (if exclusivity matters)
- Generalist digital agencies (if roofing specialization matters)
- Other lead-gen specialists that lack sales attribution

**Questions for a prospect:**
1. Do you have 2–3 months and $30K–$50K in total spend to establish the feedback loop?
2. Can you commit to weekly optimization calls and timely sales team feedback?
3. Are you OK with month-to-month billing, or do you need a long-term guarantee?
4. Can I speak with a named client (not anonymized) to verify case study claims?
